# 🌱 Empowering Sustainable Innovation – Google Solution Challenge

> Developed as part of **Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC)** using Google technologies to support the **United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).**

---

# 🌍 Project Overview

**Empowering Sustainable Innovation** is a smart environmental awareness application designed to address the improper disposal of batteries and electronic waste (E-waste), which significantly harms ecosystems and public health.

The project promotes responsible e-waste disposal, encourages recycling practices, supports a circular economy, and raises environmental awareness within communities.

By combining technology, education, and sustainability, the solution aims to reduce environmental impact and empower users to contribute to a cleaner future.

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Improper disposal of batteries and electronic components leads to serious environmental issues, including:

- Soil contamination  
- Water pollution  
- Toxic chemical leakage  
- Increased electronic waste accumulation  
- Loss of valuable recyclable materials  
- Negative impact on public health  
- Limited public awareness about safe disposal methods  

---

# 🎯 Aim of the Project

To develop a smart platform that educates users, promotes responsible E-waste disposal, and supports environmental sustainability through awareness and recycling solutions.

---

# ✅ Objectives

- Raise awareness about electronic waste hazards  
- Promote proper disposal of batteries and electronic components  
- Support recycling and circular economy practices  
- Recover valuable reusable materials  
- Reduce environmental damage caused by toxic waste  
- Educate communities about sustainability  
- Encourage environmental stewardship  
- Provide accessible eco-friendly resources
